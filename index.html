Satellite imagery on DEM terrain

Live NASA GIBS layers draped on real 3D elevation (SRTM-based)

How to read this: the 3D surface is real terrain built from a global digital elevation model. Satellite layers (MODIS / VIIRS / Landsat) are the "imagery"; the SRTM layer shows elevation tints — together they form a satellite + topographic map. Drag opacity to blend.
Imagery: NASA GIBS. Terrain: Re:Earth Mapterhorn (quantized-mesh). Viewer: CesiumJS.
